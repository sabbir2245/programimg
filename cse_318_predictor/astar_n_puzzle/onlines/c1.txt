Tile Fatigue Rule
The first time a tile is moved, its cost is equal to its face value.
The second time the same tile is moved, its cost becomes twice its face value.
The third time the same tile is moved, its cost becomes three times its face value, and so on.
For example, for tile 7:
•First movement of tile 7: cost 7
•Second movement of tile 7: cost 14
•Third movement of tile 7: cost 21
Therefore, the cost of moving a tile depends on how many times that tile has already been
moved in the current solution path.
